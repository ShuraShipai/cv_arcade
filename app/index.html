    Three ways to control a game

    Every game in the cabinet uses one of these. Pick a game below and the screen jumps to it.

    Your hands

    The webcam feeds a hand-landmark model that returns 21 points per hand. We turn those points into poses: open palm, fist, peace sign and pointing finger. The pose and where your hand sits in the frame become the game input.

    Your voice

    Scream Chicken reacts to volume: quiet stands still, talking walks and shouting jumps. Claw Machine listens for short commands—left, right, up, down and go—when local speech recognition is available.

    How a quiz question gets written

    AI Quiz Battle writes every question this way. This is what runs between your upload and the first question.

    1. Upload

      Notes, slides or a PDF go in.

    2. Split

      The text is cut into passages of about 200 words with a small overlap.

    3. Embed

      Each passage becomes a vector and goes into the index.

    4. Retrieve

      The chosen topic is embedded the same way. The closest passages come back.

    5. Write

      The LLM writes a question and four options from those passages and names the page it used.
